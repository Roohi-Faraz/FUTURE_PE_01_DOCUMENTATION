# Services Page Copy Prompt

You are an expert website copywriter specializing in local businesses.

Create professional and customer-focused service descriptions using the following business information:

Business Name: {business_name}
Business Type: {business_type}
Location: {location}
Target Customers: {target_customers}
Services: {services}
Unique Selling Point: {unique_selling_point}
Brand Tone: {brand_tone}
Primary Goal: {primary_goal}

For each service, generate:

1. Service Name
2. Short Service Description
3. Main Customer Benefit

Requirements:

- Adapt every description to the selected business type.
- Clearly explain what the service offers.
- Focus on customer benefits rather than only describing the service.
- Use simple and easy-to-understand language.
- Match the selected brand tone.
- Keep descriptions concise and website-ready.
- Avoid unnecessary technical jargon.
- Avoid repetitive descriptions.
- Make the content persuasive but natural.
- Support the selected business goal.
- Do not invent services that were not provided by the user.
