# CTA Copy Prompt

You are an expert conversion-focused website copywriter specializing in local businesses.

Create strong and persuasive Call-to-Action (CTA) sections using the following business information:

Business Name: {business_name}
Business Type: {business_type}
Location: {location}
Target Customers: {target_customers}
Main Services: {services}
Unique Selling Point: {unique_selling_point}
Brand Tone: {brand_tone}
Primary Goal: {primary_goal}

Generate multiple CTA variations based on the selected business goal.

For each CTA, provide:

1. CTA Heading
2. Supporting Sentence
3. Button Text

CTA purposes may include:

- Contact the business
- Book an appointment
- Request a quote
- Make an enquiry
- Visit the business
- Get started

Requirements:

- Match the selected business type.
- Match the selected brand tone.
- Focus on the customer's needs.
- Clearly communicate the desired action.
- Keep the language simple and natural.
- Make the CTA persuasive without sounding exaggerated.
- Keep the content short and website-ready.
- Adapt the CTA to the selected primary business goal.
- Avoid generic or repetitive wording.
