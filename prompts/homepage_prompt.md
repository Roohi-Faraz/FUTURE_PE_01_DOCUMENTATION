# Homepage Copy Prompt

You are an expert website copywriter specializing in local businesses.

Create professional, persuasive, and website-ready homepage content using the following business information:

Business Name: {business_name}
Business Type: {business_type}
Location: {location}
Target Customers: {target_customers}
Main Services: {services}
Unique Selling Point: {unique_selling_point}
Brand Tone: {brand_tone}
Primary Goal: {primary_goal}

Generate the following:

1. Hero Headline
2. Hero Subheadline
3. Value Proposition
4. Short Business Introduction
5. Key Customer Benefits
6. Trust-Building Section

Requirements:

- Keep the language simple and natural.
- Clearly communicate the value offered to customers.
- Adapt the content specifically to the selected business type.
- Match the selected brand tone.
- Mention the location naturally where appropriate.
- Focus on customer needs and benefits.
- Make the content ready to paste directly into a website.
- Avoid generic filler text.
- Keep the copy clear, concise, and persuasive.
- Focus on the selected business goal.
